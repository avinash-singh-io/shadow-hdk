# Research archive

The research files in this directory are retained as the source snapshot attached to the
`shadow-hdk` planning history. Their canonical private home is now
[`shadow-ecosystem/research`](../../../shadow-ecosystem/research/README.md).

Do not maintain both copies. New competitive research, source notes and comparative analysis go
to `shadow-ecosystem`; product architecture and accepted decisions derived from that research go
to `shadow` without importing the research corpus into the product repository.
